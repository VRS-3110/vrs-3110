BalanceBook

Your money, from your statements. BalanceBook reads the PDF bank statements Sri Lankan banks send, checks every transaction against the running balance, and keeps your totals on your own Mac, phone or computer. Made in Sri Lanka.

Everything stays on your device. BalanceBook goes online only if you connect a mailbox or sync through Google Drive, and then only to Google or Microsoft, to read your banks' statement emails or to keep sealed copies of your library in step; nothing else is uploaded.
