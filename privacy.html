BalanceBook privacy policy

BalanceBook keeps your financial data on your own device, in a file only you can read. There is no BalanceBook server, no account, and no analytics: nothing about you is collected, and nothing is sent to the people who make BalanceBook.

If you choose to connect a Gmail or Outlook mailbox, BalanceBook signs in with Google or Microsoft in your browser, asks only for permission to read mail, and reads only the emails from the bank addresses you name, to bring in their statement PDFs. The statements are read on your device and not kept. If you choose to sync through Google Drive, BalanceBook keeps encrypted copies of your library in Drive's hidden app folder, which only BalanceBook can see; Google sees only sealed files. The sign-ins are kept in your device's keychain or keystore, and Disconnect, or stopping sync, forgets them.

BalanceBook's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google or Microsoft is never shared, sold, or used for anything but showing you your own statements and keeping your own devices in step.
